# dgc.ph product brief

This page is the product brief for **dgc.ph**, the public website of De Leon Group of Companies (DGC). It says who the site is for, what it must say and which pages it has.

## Who it is for

- **Founders** building technology, digital-platform or sustainability businesses in the Philippines or Southeast Asia, who want to know whether DGC is the right partner and how to reach it.
- **Co-investors and partners** checking who DGC is, what it backs and how it works.
- **Anyone curious** about DGC or one of its portfolio companies.

## What the site must say

- DGC is a privately held, family-backed office based in Manila, Philippines.
- It invests in and builds companies across technology, digital platforms and sustainability-driven industries.
- Its capital is patient and long-term, and it partners closely with founders.
- Which companies it backs today (Bookmo, lyv, Comuni and The Hillside), with links to the live ones.
- How to get in touch: `contact@dgc.ph`.

## The four pages

| Page | Path | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | One-screen introduction, a snapshot of what DGC does, the portfolio and the investment philosophy |
| About | `/about` | Who DGC is, what it invests in and how it works with companies |
| Portfolio | `/portfolio` | The companies DGC backs, each linking to its own site |
| Contact | `/contact` | Email address and a form that drafts an email to DGC |

Every page shares the same [navigation bar and footer](manual/README.md#navigation-bar). The [user manual](manual/README.md) describes each page in detail, the [design system](design.md) describes how it looks, and the [decision log](decisions.md) records why the site is built the way it is.
